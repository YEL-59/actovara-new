Thank you for the latest update and for incorporating the changes from our frontend review checklist.

Following my initial review, I have two additional points:

1. Future Mobile App / Property Inspections

I'd like to explore whether Actovara could eventually support mobile site inspections, allowing property managers to:

- Select a property and complete inspection checklists.
- Record findings and upload photographs.
- Create corrective actions directly from inspections.
- Assign responsibilities, priorities and deadlines.
- Save inspection records against the property's history.

I appreciate this is outside the current MVP scope and am not requesting additional development at this stage.

However, could you advise whether our current architecture would support this in Phase 2, potentially starting with a mobile-responsive web application before developing a dedicated app?

2. Closing Corrective Actions

During my review, I couldn't close an action directly from the Actions tab.

My understanding is that the workflow should be:

Open → In Progress → Awaiting Verification → Closed

An authorised Organisation Admin should be able to review completion evidence, verify and close an action, with the approver and date recorded.

Ideally, this should be accessible from the Actions tab or the individual action record. There should also be an option to request further evidence or reopen an action with a recorded reason.

Could you confirm whether this functionality is working and demonstrate the intended process?

As corrective action verification is central to Actovara's Know. Act. Prove. approach, I consider this part of the existing MVP rather than an additional feature.

Please let me know your thoughts on these points so we can complete the frontend review and progress to the next stage.
